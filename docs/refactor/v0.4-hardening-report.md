# AIO-Gym 0.4 post-refactor hardening report

Date: 2026-08-07

## Scope and outcome

This release keeps the five-package Scenario -> PlantConfig ->
OperatingCondition -> TaskSpec -> Environment architecture and closes six
identity or semantics gaps:

1. Plant, Condition, Task objective, and resolved-plant mappings are deeply
   immutable; declared hashes are validated.
2. Engineering studies share one explicit resolved Condition across static,
   steady, dynamic, safety, robustness, and sweep paths.
3. Parameterized three-tank public actions contain only installed actuators.
4. Evaluation and collection share one strict policy-target contract resolver.
5. Scheduled events are applied before the corresponding policy action and
   Dataset v4 records transition and next-observation contexts.
6. The economic Task uses dimensional rates integrated over `control_dt`, with
   coefficients included in Task identity.

## Intentional compatibility changes

- package version: 0.4.0;
- Dataset: v4 with `pre-action-v1` semantics;
- evaluation result schema: v2;
- economic Task: revision 2, `net-economic-value-v2`, `economic-core-v2`;
- custom equipment layout changes alter Plant and interface hashes;
- old economic and mismatched-interface checkpoints are rejected;
- the CasADi extra is `symbolic`; unimplemented ONNX/HPO extras are removed.

## Validation

The final verification used CPython 3.14.6 with NumPy 2.5.0, Gymnasium 1.3.0,
CasADi 3.7.2, Stable-Baselines3 2.9.0, and Torch 2.13.0.

### Scenario and workflow matrix

- The quadruple model and each built-in three-tank Plant complete environment
  construction, reset, fixed-action stepping, PID and MPC rollout, one-episode
  collection, and DatasetReader checksum validation.
- The H1, H1+H3, and H1+H2+H3 parameterized layouts expose 4, 5, and 6 public
  actions respectively. Their Plant/interface hashes differ, controller and
  Dataset action dimensions match, and numeric/CasADi dynamics and energy
  calculations agree.
- Custom Conditions remain bound across static, steady-state, dynamic, safety,
  robustness, and sweep checks. Short horizons return the explicit
  `insufficient_assessment_horizon` status.
- Scheduled events at reset and step boundaries are visible to the action that
  follows them. Dataset v4 records separate transition and next-observation
  condition/reference/disturbance contexts using `pre-action-v1` semantics.
- SAC trains, saves, reloads, evaluates, and collects on the supported
  environments. PPO, TD3, and DDPG complete the two-step save/load smoke path.
  Condition transfer is rejected by default and recorded when explicitly
  allowed; Task/interface mismatches remain rejected.
- The open-cascade economic objective reports dimensional product value,
  energy cost, production volume in m3, and energy in kWh. Its integration is
  stable for equal physical durations across different control intervals.

These are pipeline and semantic smoke checks, not control-performance claims.

### Distribution verification

`python -m build` produced:

- `dist/aiogym-0.4.0-py3-none-any.whl`;
- `dist/aiogym-0.4.0.tar.gz`.

Both artifacts pass `python -m twine check`. Archive inspection confirms that
they contain the three built-in three-tank Plant JSON files, README/license,
and package metadata, and contain none of `tests/`, cache/bytecode files,
`runs/`, generated `*.egg-info/`, or legacy cascade scenario packages. A
setuptools sdist hook removes the otherwise auto-generated egg-info source list
from the release tree.

The wheel was installed with dependencies into
`/tmp/aiogym-v040-wheel-test` and passed `pip check`. From `/tmp`, it reports
version 0.4.0 and exactly the `quadruple` and `three_tank` scenarios; core import
does not load Torch or Stable-Baselines3. Wheel-only PID evaluation and Dataset
collection passed for quadruple plus open-cascade-v1, recirculating-h1-v1, and
lab-three-tank-v1.

Final artifact SHA-256 values are generated after the archive is complete in
`dist/SHA256SUMS`. The sdist checksum is intentionally kept outside the sdist,
because an archive cannot embed its own final cryptographic checksum.

## Remaining limitations

- Validation was local on CPython 3.14.6; the checked-in CI matrix covers
  CPython 3.10 through 3.13 but no remote CI run is claimed here.
- RL runs use two training steps and demonstrate wiring, serialization, and
  identity enforcement only. They do not demonstrate learned control quality.
- MPC checks are short functional rollouts, not formal-horizon benchmark or
  robustness claims.
- The parameterized laboratory Plant values remain design-provisional and
  require calibration against installed equipment before physical deployment.
